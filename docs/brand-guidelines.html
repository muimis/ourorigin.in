01 · Brand Overview

What OurOrigin Is

The definition
A counting system.
A proof system.
A ledger.

OurOrigin is not a spice company, not a Western Ghats brand, not a premium food label. It is a proof system — documenting that something real happened between a place and the people who understood it, and that what reached the buyer's hand is the evidence of that fact.

Every product has: a coordinate, a batch reference, a community name, and a season. These are not marketing copy. They are the product.

02 · The Mark

The Tally Gate

On Void · Primary
On Cream · Reversed
On Dark · Cream stroke
01
The Four Verticals
Four strokes — slightly imperfect, slightly organic. Not machine-made. The same mark carved into cave walls, prison cells, harvest records. Counting something real.
02
The Diagonal
The fifth stroke closes the group. The gate. A completed cycle. In OurOrigin's context: a batch counted, a harvest verified, a record closed.
03
The Imperfection
The curves are intentional. No SVG straight lines. These marks were drawn by a human. That is the point — it is proof of a human act, not a digital symbol.
04
The Absence of Decoration
No leaf. No mountain. No grain. The mark is the concept, not an illustration of the inventory. "Counted, verified, recorded" — said by the form alone.

06 · Photography

Two languages. No exceptions.

PRODUCT
VOID · MUSEUM LIT
TYPE I · THE ARTIFACT
The Artifact
  • Product suspended in void black. No surface, no shadow, no context object.
  • Museum lighting — single directional, high contrast, dramatic.
  • Hyper-real detail. The product is the only subject.
  • No packaging in artifact shots — product only.
  • Post-processing: deep blacks, lifted mid-tones, minimal saturation.
𝄿
HANDS · CHIAROSCURO
TYPE II · THE GESTURE
The Gesture
  • Hands only. Never a face, never a portrait, never lifestyle.
  • Chiaroscuro — deep contrast, dramatic shadow. Not documentary.
  • The hands interact with the product or the land. Never posed formally.
  • No bracelets, painted nails, or styled jewelry in frame.
  • Convert to near-monochrome with orange-toned highlights only.
Never use these
Lifestyle / table setting
Portrait / face photography

12 · What Not To Do

Institutional memory — so these don't get re-proposed. Every item here was considered and rejected.

✕
OurOrigin
Gold color + circle mark
Gold reads precious, wellness, luxury. The circular mark was the rejected crosshair. Both gone.
✕
OurOrigin
Mark in rounded container
No border-radius anywhere. The mark lives in void, not in a badge or pill shape.
✕
Green backgrounds
Green = organic certification, eco brand, sustainability claim. Not what this is.
✕
"Crafted with love and care by indigenous artisans in the lush valleys..."
Narrative copy without facts
Every sentence must have a verifiable fact. Feeling-language without coordinates is a claim.
✕
OurOrigin
Serif wordmark alone
The mark IS the logo. A wordmark without the tally gate loses all brand concept. Always pair.
✕
Glow / neon effects on mark
The mark is a carving, a scratch, a count. No glow. No digital effects. Raw stroke only.
