# Products Framework
Currently we keep 1 variation of the same product, priced comparably to the premium category in Kerala (incorporating web source rates). We are removing "isAnchorProduct" and "harvestMonth" complexities.

## Foxtail Millet
- **Origin**: Nilgiris, Western Ghats
- **Price**: ₹250 / 500g
- **Description**: A drought-resistant ancient grain, deeply rooted in the indigenous farming traditions. Low GI, high protein.

## Little Millet
- **Origin**: Wayanad, Western Ghats
- **Price**: ₹220 / 500g
- **Description**: Sourced from the high altitudes. Extremely dense in micronutrients. 

## Finger Millet (Ragi)
- **Origin**: Attappady, Western Ghats
- **Price**: ₹180 / 500g
- **Description**: The staple powerhouse of the ghats. High calcium, slow-digesting, pure yield.
