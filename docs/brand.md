# OurOrigin — Brand

---

## What It Is

OurOrigin is a counting system.

The farmer counts harvests in tally marks. The land counts seasons. We count batches. Everything sold here has a number, a name, a coordinate, and a community behind it.

That specificity is the product. The spice, the millet, the tea — those are the proof.

---

## What It Is Not

Not ethical sourcing. Not sustainable farming. Not premium artisan.

Those are claims. This is a record.

We don't empower farmers through charity. We record the fact of their labor accurately and pay what it's worth. The dignity is already there. We didn't put it there. We just stopped erasing it.

## The Mark

```
| | | | /
```

The tally gate. Four verticals crossed by one diagonal.

The oldest proof-of-count on earth. Farmers use it. Prisoners use it. Anyone who needs to record something true without a computer uses it.

It closes a cycle. A gate of five means something was counted and completed. Not ongoing, not aspirational — done.

That is the OurOrigin mark.

**Why not a logo with a leaf, a mountain, a grain?**
Those are illustrations of things we sell. A logo should be the concept, not the inventory. The tally mark IS the concept: counted, verified, recorded.

## The Color

**Surveyor's Orange — `#FF4500`**

The color of a marking stake in the ground before a survey begins. A field instrument tape. The circle drawn on a map before anyone else has arrived.

Not gold (precious, curated, luxury).
Not green (organic certification energy).
Not red (warning, brand aggression).

Surveyor's Orange on void black: a live signal. Something active in the field. A harvest in progress.

## Voice & Tone

**We speak in facts, not feelings.**

| Say this | Not this |
|---|---|
| `11°36′N, 76°04′E` | "nestled in the lush hills of..." |
| `Indigenous farming communities, Wayanad` | "our partner farmers who..." |
| `Harvested hand-selected, sun-dried on traditional mats` | "crafted with love and care" |
| `Batch WYD-BP-001` | "small-batch, artisan" |
| `500–1,100m elevation` | "high-altitude quality" |
| `Whole stalk harvest, hand-winnowed` | "traditional methods" |

**One rule:** If a sentence could appear on any food brand's website, cut it or make it specific.

**Tone registers:**
- **Evidence** — Coordinates, batch codes, elevation. No decoration. DM Mono.
- **Narrative** — The one sentence about method. Still spare. Cormorant Garamond italic.
- **Statement** — Product name. Section header. Cinzel. No adjectives.

## What We Never Say

- "Empowering communities" — they were never powerless. We just recorded accurately.
- "Sustainable" — a claim that requires no proof. We give proof instead.
- "Artisan" — this is industrial language dressed in craft.
- "Premium" — the product's price is a fact. Its provenance is the value.
- "Ethical sourcing" — ethics is not a feature. Accuracy is.
- "From farm to table" — the most erased phrase in food.
- "100% natural" — as opposed to what?

## The Seven States of an Origin

Every origin OurOrigin sources from is verified across seven layers. None outranks the other.

1. **Geo** — One exact coordinate. Not a region. A place.
2. **Bio** — The land's own ecology before any human arrives.
3. **Community** — The people who have understood this place longer than we've known it exists.
4. **Method** — How they take from the land without breaking it.
5. **Observation** — What is happening there right now, not last year's photograph.
6. **Time** — The season, the monsoon cycle, the specific harvest window.
7. **Product** — What reaches your hand. Proof that all six above were true.

If any of the seven is absent, the origin is not ready to enter the archive.

## Three Visitor Paths

None is the "right" path. All are designed to be complete.

1. **Buy and go** — Lands on product. Sensory hits first. Buys. Done. Never forced through philosophy.
2. **Explore** — Follows the coordinates into field notes and origin narratives. Depth available on request.
3. **Refer** — Multi-origin order. Sends the ledger link to someone else. The record persists.

## Design Principles (Locked)

- **No border-radius.** Brutalist. Deliberate edges only.
- **No gradients.** Except the header fade — functional, not decorative.
- **No glassmorphism.** We are not a SaaS product.
- **Two type densities only:** Wide (narrative) and Dense (evidence). Never mixed in the same block.
- **Whitespace is a decision.** Not a leftover. Empty space on screen = "this record is complete."
- **The product image is the artifact.** Museum-lit, suspended in void, no lifestyle.
- **Hands only, never faces.** The Gesture: chiaroscuro. No portrait. No stock.
- **The tally mark appears everywhere it counts something.** Cart count, archive status, section dividers.

## What Was Rejected (Identity)

Kept as institutional memory — so these don't get re-proposed.

- Vesica piscis (two overlapping circles) — overused in organic/wellness.
- Leaf-and-seed woven mark — multi-concept, not one reduced idea.
- Ring thinning to a point — reads as loading spinner.
- Topographic/map imagery — cold. "Selling land, not life."
- Tribal/ethnic illustration — appropriation risk.
- Crosshair/target mark — generic, military energy.
- Gold color — too precious for a proof system. Surveyor's Orange instead.
- Philosophy-first homepage — visitors come for product, not manifesto.
