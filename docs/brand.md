# OurOrigin — Brand Bible

## What OurOrigin Is

OurOrigin is not a spice company. It is not a Western Ghats brand. It is not a premium food label.

OurOrigin is a proof system. It proves that something real happened between a place and the person who understood it — and that what ended up in your hand is the evidence.

The Western Ghats is where the proof currently comes from. That is a fact about today's inventory, not a definition of what OurOrigin is.

---

## The Soul

> "The product is not the soul. The land is not the soul. The soul is the profound, invisible exchange of dignity and effort from one human being to another. The farmer puts an invaluable amount of ethics and effort into the yield. The buyer honors that effort. The money is just the mechanism that completes the circuit of respect."

## The Seven-States Model

An origin is seven interconnected things. None ranks above another.

1. **Geo** — Every true thing comes from one exact place. Not a region. A place.
2. **Bio** — Before anyone arrives to take anything, the place already has a life of its own.
3. **Community** — Someone has understood this place longer than we've known it exists.
4. **Method** — There is a way of taking, and a way of taking too much. This is the difference we look for.
5. **Observation** — Not a photograph from last year. What's happening there, right now.
6. **Time** — It has seasons. It changes. What you're looking at is alive, not preserved.
7. **Product** — What ends up in your hand is proof all of this was true. Or it's nothing.

## The Two Scales

Everything OurOrigin says is true at two scales simultaneously:

- **The Specific** — One named community, one origin, a real distance actually walked.
- **The Universal** — The same origin story behind every culture's relationship to labor, since before money existed.

## Ethics

### The Harvest
- Take only what's ripe.
- Leave what the animals have claimed.
- Leave what the season hasn't finished.
- Carry back only what was agreed.

### The Business
- **The origin remains the protagonist** — never stripped of context to be "easier to consume."
- **No exploitation of vulnerability** — no pseudo-charity marketing. No photographing poverty.
- **Connection over commerce** — the market is the final inch, not the end of the story.

### The Community (The First Hands)
- **The First Hands** (plural) — the specific, named harvester community. Used at product/origin level.
- **The First Hand** (singular) — the universal, unplaced, ancient register. Used once, at the widest angle.
- Communities are named generically until specific names are verified *and* consented to: `"Indigenous farming communities, [Region]"`

## The Governing Principle

> "When you ask 'why this?', the answer must always point back to the source. If any decision doesn't reflect the dignity of the walk or the legacy of the community, it's the wrong decision."

## What a Visitor Should Feel, In Order

1. **Curiosity** — "What are these people actually talking about?"
2. **Recognition** — "Oh — this is about proving something is real, not selling something."
3. **Grounding** — "Right now, what they can prove is the Western Ghats — here's how."
4. **Choice** — Buy, or explore, or both. None forced.

## Three User Journeys

1. **Buy and go** — Knows what they want. Never routed through philosophy. Direct add.
2. **Explore and understand** — Wants the full descent. The Depth Experience.
3. **Accumulate and refer** — Multi-origin order, then tells someone else. The Ledger persists.

None is the "main" path.

## What Was Tried and Rejected (Identity)

- Vesica piscis (two overlapping circles) — overused in organic/wellness.
- Leaf-and-seed woven — multi-concept mixture, not one reduced idea.
- Ring thinning to a point — loading spinner.
- Literal topographic/map imagery — cold. "Selling land, not life."
- Tribal/ethnic illustration — appropriation risk.
