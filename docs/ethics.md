# Ethics & Community

## The Framework
Our ethics are governed by the land. Every decision must answer the "What, When, How, Why" test.
- **What** are we harvesting? Only what the soil naturally yields.
- **When** do we harvest? In strict alignment with monsoon cycles.
- **How** do we process? Low-temperature curing, minimal intervention.
- **Why** do we do it? To build a ledger of truth, proving the origin of every grain.

## Community
Our community is the First Hands. The families in Nilgiris, Idukki, Wayanad, Attappady, and Thiruvananthapuram. We don't "empower" them through charity; we empower the truth of their labor through immutable ledgers and fair, direct pricing.
